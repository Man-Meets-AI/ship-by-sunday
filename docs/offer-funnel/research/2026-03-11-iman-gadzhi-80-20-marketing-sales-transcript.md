## Applied Takeaways For Ship by Sunday

- Own a named mechanism instead of describing the offer as generic coaching or generic information.
- Use the mechanism to explain why the buyer keeps getting stuck.
- Make the offer feel like a better process, not just more content.
- Name the problem in simple language that a non-expert could understand.
- Surface the limiting beliefs the mechanism creates, then answer them in the offer.
- Use bonuses to reduce fear without making the core product feel bloated.
- For us, that means centering `The Product Trinity`, presenting `Ship by Sunday` as the workspace and sprint, and using `Product Management for Beginners` as a fear-reducing bonus for weak product judgment.
