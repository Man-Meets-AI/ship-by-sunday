# Social Variants Example

## Source message

Most AI builds do not fail because the model is weak. They fail because the context drifts.

## Variant 1

Most AI builds start with prompts.
The part that breaks first is the context.
Ship by Sunday starts with product truth, then turns it into design, marketing, build, and prompt packs.

## Variant 2

If your product truth lives in five stale chats, the build will drift.
Ship by Sunday is built to lock the facts first.

## Variant 3

The starter is not the product.
The blueprint is the product.
The starter just gives that truth a real place to keep shipping from.

## Guardrail

Do not add proof, price, or guarantee language in short-form social unless it has been explicitly approved for that run.
