# Ship by Sunday Marketing Pack

This folder is the Ship by Sunday-specific marketing pack layered on top of `docs/openai/openai-marketing-kit/`.

It is the template for the customer-facing `docs/marketing/` codebase that should be generated into the codebase after the review route.

## What this pack is for

- Turn product facts into a usable marketing codebase for prompts, workflows, and reviewable deliverables.
- Make approved claims, channel scope, human review rules, and current-facts policy explicit before content generation starts.
- Keep marketing work aligned to the same product truth used by design and build.

## What is in scope

- marketing goals and channel scope
- brand voice summary
- approved claims and do-not-say rules
- research needs, content workflows, and analytics needs
- workflow plans for launch assets, research, and review loops
- documentation of live marketing workflows and governance

## What is out of scope

- unverified current facts presented as certainty
- auto-publishing external content without human review
- pricing, guarantees, or customer claims that were not approved in the facts layer
- inventing target channels, audiences, or positioning not supported by the codebase facts
- product or design scope changes disguised as marketing work

## Files in this pack

- `README.md` - what the marketing codebase is for
- `AGENTS.md` - reusable marketing agent and workflow rules
- `CHECKLIST.md` - marketing capability and governance checklist
- `PLANS.md` - rollout plan for marketing workflows and deliverables
- `documentation.md` - shipped workflow and governance documentation
- `claims.md` - approved claims, verify-first claims, and forbidden claim patterns
- `review-matrix.md` - what can be drafted, what must be reviewed, and what must never auto-publish
- `deliverables.md` - the actual marketing artifacts this codebase is meant to support
- `assets/` - lift-ready copy assets for VSL, short close, and other direct-response pieces
- `assets/writing-pass.md` - hard rewrite gate for making copy sound owned instead of generic
- `examples/launch-message-kit.md` - a concrete golden-example codebase
- `examples/homepage-message-hierarchy.md` - example homepage narrative output
- `examples/launch-email-outline.md` - example launch email output
- `examples/social-variants.md` - example social adaptation output
- `examples/porch-close.md` - short close copy shown as a worked example
- `examples/vsl-script.md` - cold open, opener, and full VSL draft shown as a worked example
