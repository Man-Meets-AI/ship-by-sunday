# Ship by Sunday Marketing Checklist

Use this checklist to gate a customer-product marketing packet before it is treated as usable.

## 1. Core inputs

- [ ] Marketing goals are explicit.
- [ ] Channel scope is explicit.
- [ ] Brand voice summary exists.
- [ ] Approved claims are listed.
- [ ] Do-not-say rules are listed.
- [ ] Review rules are explicit.
- [ ] Current-facts policy is explicit.

## 2. Workflow inventory

- [ ] Campaign types are named.
- [ ] Research needs are named.
- [ ] Content workflows are named.
- [ ] Analytics needs are named.
- [ ] Stable knowledge and fresh inputs are clearly separated.
- [ ] Output contracts exist for the workflows that matter.

## 3. Governance

- [ ] The pack says what requires human review.
- [ ] The pack says what can be drafted without approval.
- [ ] The pack says what must never be auto-published.
- [ ] Current-fact verification is required wherever necessary.
- [ ] Brand-risk and legal-risk areas are called out.

## 4. Practical usefulness

- [ ] A marketer could use this packet without rereading the whole intake history.
- [ ] The channel list is narrow enough to be actionable.
- [ ] The claims are specific enough to write from.
- [ ] The restrictions are clear enough to prevent bad copy drift.
- [ ] The workflows are tied to real deliverables, not generic marketing busywork.
