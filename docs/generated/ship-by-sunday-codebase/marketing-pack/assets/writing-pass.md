# Writing Pass

Rewrite this with the following prompt pass:

## Writing Rules

- Write like a sharp human, not a language model.
- Use contractions naturally.
- Short paragraphs, 1 to 3 sentences max.
- Get to the point. No throat-clearing, no preamble.
- If making a claim, be specific. Use numbers, names, and concrete details.
- Vary sentence length. Mix short punchy lines with longer ones.
- Use natural transitions, not mechanical ones.
- When uncertain, say so plainly.
- Never pad output to seem more thorough. Shorter and accurate beats longer and fluffy.
- Use physical verbs for abstract processes.
- Humor comes from specificity, not from jokes.
- Parenthetical asides are good when they feel honest.

## Formatting Rules

- Short paragraphs, 1 to 2 sentences by default, 3 max.
- Numbers as digits.
- Contractions always.
- No em dashes ever.
- Bold sparingly, 1 to 2 key moments per section.
- Code blocks for specific prompts, commands, or tool outputs.

## Banned Phrases

### Dead AI language

- "In today's [anything]..."
- "It's important to note that..."
- "It's worth noting..."
- "Delve"
- "Dive into"
- "Unpack"
- "Harness"
- "Leverage"
- "Utilize"
- "Landscape"
- "Realm"
- "Robust"
- "Game-changer"
- "Cutting-edge"
- "Straightforward"
- "I'd be happy to help"
- "In order to"

### Dead transitions

- "Furthermore"
- "Additionally"
- "Moreover"
- "Moving forward"
- "At the end of the day"
- "To put this in perspective..."
- "What makes this particularly interesting is..."
- "The implications here are..."
- "In other words..."
- "It goes without saying..."

### Engagement bait

- "Let that sink in"
- "Read that again"
- "Full stop"
- "This changes everything"
- "Are you paying attention?"
- "You're not ready for this"

### AI cringe

- "Supercharge"
- "Unlock"
- "Future-proof"
- "10x your productivity"
- "The AI revolution"
- "In the age of AI"

### Generic insider claims

- "Here's the part nobody's talking about"
- "What nobody tells you"
- anything with "nobody" or "most people don't realize"

### Fatal pattern

- "This isn't X. This is Y."
- "Not X. Y."
- "Forget X. This is Y."
- "Less X, more Y."
- any sentence that negates one framing and then asserts a corrected one

If even 1 of these appears, the output fails. Delete the negation and just state the positive claim.

## Preferred traits

- direct claims
- concrete nouns
- uneven rhythm
- sharp verbs
- specific details
- real tradeoffs
- mild opinion when appropriate

If a sentence sounds polished but interchangeable, rewrite it until it feels owned.
