# Ship by Sunday YouTube Launch Plan

Last updated: 2026-03-07

## Goal

Use YouTube to build trust and urgency for `Ship by Sunday` without drifting into generic AI workflow content.

This plan is intentionally narrow. It is for launch support, not for building a giant content machine.

## Audience

- junior and mid-level engineers
- can code
- have ideas
- keep not finishing
- want proof, momentum, and a clear system

## Recommended first credibility video

### Video objective

Show that the founder understands the real problem better than generic AI-builder content does.

### Audience segment

Warm or adjacent builders who keep starting and stopping.

### Title options

- `Why smart engineers still do not ship apps`
- `The real reason your app never gets finished`
- `Most unfinished apps die in the same place`

### Hook options

- `If you can code, have ideas, and still do not get anything live, your problem probably is not skill. It is sequence.`
- `Most unfinished apps do not die because the founder is lazy. They die because the path is muddy from the start.`
- `The messy middle kills more apps than the hard parts do.`

### Thumbnail idea

- founder face or direct on-camera still
- short phrase like `YOU STILL DON'T SHIP`
- black / white high-contrast treatment
- one visual cue tied to incomplete work, repo graveyard, or stuck momentum

### CTA path

- early verbal fit check: this is for engineers who can code but do not finish
- mid-video introduce the 4-block idea
- end CTA: `If you want the full system, check out Ship by Sunday`

### Why this should go first

- it sells the problem, not just the product
- it qualifies the buyer fast
- it positions the offer as a finishing system
- it can be recorded without a giant demo setup

## Concept 2

### Video objective

Make the 4-block system feel concrete and teachable.

### Audience segment

People who are interested but need more structure before they buy.

### Title options

- `The 4-block system I use to go from idea to v1`
- `How to go from idea to v1 without spiraling`
- `A better way to build with AI when you keep getting stuck`

### Hook options

- `If your workflow is just bouncing between prompts, screenshots, and half-built repos, you do not have a system yet.`
- `The way most people build with AI creates chaos. Here is the sequence that reduces it.`

### Thumbnail idea

- 4-block graphic
- simple phrase like `IDEA -> V1`
- no tool collage

### CTA path

- walk through the four blocks
- show the four artifacts
- end CTA: `Ship by Sunday turns this into a guided weekend rep`

## Concept 3

### Video objective

Show a believable before-and-after path from unfinished idea to shipped v1.

### Audience segment

People who want possibility plus proof.

### Title options

- `I turned an unfinished idea into a shipped v1 with AI`
- `From half-started idea to live app`
- `What it actually takes to ship a v1 this weekend`

### Hook options

- `Most people show you the finished app. I want to show you the path that gets you there.`
- `The interesting part is not the final demo. It is how you stop the idea from dying halfway through.`

### Thumbnail idea

- split-screen before / after
- phrase like `IDEA TO LIVE`
- one screenshot of the finished product

### CTA path

- show the scope discipline and system
- avoid overpromising giant app outcomes
- end CTA: `If you want the weekend version of this path, Ship by Sunday is the system`

## Thumbnail direction

- bold, sparse, editorial
- one idea per thumbnail
- no generic “AI tools everywhere” visual style
- keep the frame emotionally legible at a glance
- strong copy options:
  - `YOU STILL DON'T SHIP`
  - `IDEA -> V1`
  - `WHY YOU GET STUCK`
  - `SHIP THIS WEEKEND`

## CTA logic by audience temperature

### Cold viewer

- sell the problem and worldview first
- CTA: `See how Ship by Sunday works`

### Warm viewer

- sell the structure and proof
- CTA: `Get Instant Access`

### Buyer-adjacent viewer

- focus on fit, scope, and what they will actually leave with
- CTA: `Go from idea to v1 this weekend`

## What to avoid

- generic tool roundup videos
- “top 10 AI tools” content
- workflow porn without an outcome
- overlong technical build logs before the offer is live
- vague motivational creator talk

## Production order

1. `Why smart engineers still do not ship apps`
2. `The 4-block system I use to go from idea to v1`
3. `I turned an unfinished idea into a shipped v1 with AI`

## Definition of done

This lane is done for now when:

- one first credibility video is chosen
- three launch-fit concepts exist
- title and hook options are ready
- thumbnail direction is clear
- each concept has a clean CTA path into Ship by Sunday
