# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
The primary user is a knowledge worker using AI coding and design tools to build an app. They open Ship by Sunday when they want a serious build environment that helps them move from an idea to a shipped v1 in one weekend. They are not looking for entertainment or hand-holding. They want leverage, momentum, and a system they can trust while making fast product decisions.

### Brand Personality
The brand personality is curious, bold, and serious. The interface should feel excited, confident, and expensive. It should communicate that real work is happening here: strong product judgment, clear constraints, and high standards. The tone should feel premium and deliberate rather than playful, cute, or casual.

### Aesthetic Direction
The visual direction should sit between the simplicity of Airbnb and the minimalism of early Uber, adapted to this product's more technical and agent-driven use case. Keep the existing strong display typography, sharp section framing, restrained palettes, and high-contrast hierarchy. Use motion and interaction polish intentionally, especially where the product already has strong taste markers like pointer highlights, dramatic headlines, proof surfaces, and system diagrams.

Design for both light and dark mode as first-class experiences. Avoid childish or mascot-driven aesthetics, including Duolingo-like fonts, soft toy-like styling, or anything that makes the product feel juvenile. The interface should feel clean, modern, premium, and operational, not whimsical.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with decisive hierarchy. Make the most important idea immediately obvious through scale, contrast, and spacing.
2. Make the product feel premium, not decorative. Use bold typography, clean surfaces, and restrained color so the interface feels expensive and serious.
3. Preserve interaction signatures. Reuse existing distinctive patterns like pointer highlights, strong visual framing, and intentional motion instead of flattening them into generic SaaS UI.
4. Design for momentum. Every screen should help a builder feel forward motion from idea to shipped v1, not friction, clutter, or indecision.
5. Stay minimal without becoming sterile. Remove noise, but keep enough personality and tension that the product still feels bold and memorable.
