# Code of Conduct

This project expects respectful, practical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in technical disagreement.
- Assume good intent, then ask for clarity when something is ambiguous.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and tied to the work.
- Share context that helps others reproduce issues and understand tradeoffs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or conduct
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- Repeated bad-faith arguing, trolling, or disruptive behavior
- Sexual language or unwanted sexual attention

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent removal from the project spaces.

## Reporting

If you run into a conduct issue, contact the maintainers privately instead of escalating it in public threads.

Include:

- what happened
- links or screenshots if relevant
- when it happened
- who was involved

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible.
